I researched this again using current 2026 signals from Gartner, Visa, Microsoft, NVIDIA, the Bank of England, the World Economic Forum, and others. My conclusion is somewhat different from my first answer.

My strongest prediction: The Agentic Internet → Physical AI

The next major evolution probably isn't simply “more powerful AI.”

It is the transition from:

AI that answers → AI that acts → AI systems that coordinate → AI that controls the physical world.

In other words:

> The internet was built for humans.
The next internet will increasingly be built for agents.



And after that, those agents increasingly connect to the physical world.

1. We are still in the middle of the Agentic AI transition

This is important because I don't think “AI Agents” should already be treated as the previous era.

Gartner predicted that 40% of enterprise applications would have task-specific AI agents by the end of 2026, compared with less than 5% in 2025. It also expects collaborative agents and eventually agent ecosystems spanning multiple applications. 

Microsoft's 2025 research similarly described a new type of organization built around human + AI-agent teams, with 81% of surveyed leaders expecting agents to be moderately or extensively integrated into their AI strategy within 12–18 months. 

So:

2025–27 = Agent adoption

But that's not the end state.

2. The next layer is Agent-to-Agent infrastructure

This is where I think the story gets much bigger.

Today:

Human → AI Agent → Application

The emerging architecture is:

Human → Agent A ↔ Agent B ↔ Agent C → Applications/APIs

Google created the Agent2Agent protocol, and the Linux Foundation launched the A2A project in 2025 specifically to enable agents from different vendors and frameworks to communicate and collaborate. 

Anthropic's MCP is solving another part of the problem: standardized connections between AI systems and the tools/data where work actually happens. 

And Gartner expects agent ecosystems across applications by 2028, with agents dynamically collaborating across multiple applications and business functions. 

That is a much bigger shift than simply putting an AI chatbot inside a banking app.


---

3. Then comes something even bigger: AI becomes the customer

This is the part I would pay serious attention to.

Visa is explicitly describing the transition as:

human commerce → e-commerce → mobile commerce → agentic commerce

Its 2026 research says AI agents are already beginning to search, compare, negotiate and transact, and it expects agentic commerce to become a major new commerce paradigm. 

Visa reports that 53% of surveyed businesses would allow AI agents to negotiate prices or terms directly with other AI agents. 

That's profound.

The web historically optimized for:

> Humans browsing websites.



The next web may optimize for:

> Agents selecting and negotiating with businesses.



Imagine an AI buying software, cloud compute, advertising, travel, inventory, insurance or financial services on behalf of a company.

The company doesn't necessarily visit 50 websites.

Its agent does.

4. And this is already reaching banking/payments

This is why I think your original banking example is actually an excellent way to think about the future.

Visa announced a 2026 partnership with OpenAI aimed at enabling secure Visa payments within agentic commerce. Their infrastructure includes payment credentials, tokenization, authorization, agent identification and fraud monitoring. 

Visa also launched/expanded its Agentic Ready program for banks and payment partners, specifically so financial institutions can test and prepare for agent-initiated transactions. 

And in September 2026, Reuters reported that India is preparing a framework for agentic payments through UPI, including spending limits, identity checks and delegated funds. 

So the banking evolution may look like:

Banking website → Mobile banking → AI banking assistant → Banking agent → Agentic banking infrastructure

The bank isn't merely giving you an AI assistant.

It has to become infrastructure that other AI agents can safely interact with.

The World Economic Forum describes physical AI as robotic systems capable of perception, reasoning and autonomous action.
